## Executive Summary

CognitiveCore is a directive-governed cognitive substrate for long-running creative and strategic work. Its design treats identity, canon, human intent, and memory permanence as first-class constraints rather than as undifferentiated chat history.

The central product thesis is straightforward: **generated material should remain provisional until a human decides that it has earned a place in the work**. The architecture therefore places quarantine, evaluation, contradiction handling, and human approval between generation and durable memory.

This is an acquisition opportunity in architecture, behavioral contracts, governance patterns, and a defined remediation path. It is not yet a revenue-generating SaaS business or a validated performance leader.

## What the Prototype Includes

| Capability | Description |

## Real Moat Versus Cosmetic Differentiation

The substantive differentiators are the behavioral contract—intention, identity, memory permanence, conflict as signal, and directive-driven next action—the quarantine boundary, incident memory as an adaptive constitutional layer, and a builder-facing Creative Constitution model.

Field-simulation metaphors, hash or theme embeddings, and dashboard metrics without write-back gates are comparatively easy to reproduce. The durable value therefore resides in the integrated governance loop and its operational discipline, not in terminology or presentation alone.

## Principal Risks

A competent buyer should price the following risks. The canon and metric proof gap requires real-model reruns on a frozen corpus with raw outputs. The proxy contradiction layer may miss novel paraphrases. The package appears dependent on a key maintainer, making a contractual transition window important. Prior overclaiming or extreme valuation narratives should be retired from the data room. Finally, dependency, license, API-client, embedding-library, and code-lineage audits remain necessary.

## 90-Day Validation and Product Path

1. Run the frozen corpus on real language models and publish methodology, anonymized outputs, and reproducible receipts.
2. Place the LLM judge on the contradiction escalation path while retaining the proxy as a cost-saving prefilter.
3. Implement project isolation, a constitution editor, and a quarantine inbox.
4. Add durable persistence and audit export.
5. Produce a side-by-side demonstration showing constitution, violation, protection, and regeneration against a conventional baseline.
